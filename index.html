🗺️ The Parade Route

Follow the peppermint path! The parade winds through Historic Downtown Garden City along Main Street.

Good to know

  • Main Street closes to traffic at 5:00 PM. Plan to arrive early and park on side streets.
  • The best viewing spots are along Main Street between Kansas Avenue and Laurel Street.
  • Watch for the Cocoa Stop for free hot chocolate while supplies last.
  • Stick around after the finale for the Community Tree Lighting downtown.
  • Accessible viewing areas are available. Look for the volunteers in candy cane vests!

⏰ Evening Schedule

A full evening of sugar-plum fun, from the first jingle to the final glow.

  1. 4:00 PM

    Candy Cane Lane Opens

    Downtown vendors, hot cocoa, and holiday music kick off the fun. Visit with Santa's elves and pick up your free candy cane.

  2. 4:30 PM

    Float Line-Up

    Parade entries assemble at the staging area. Judges make their rounds for the float awards.

  3. 5:30 PM

    Caroling on the Corner

    Local school choirs warm up the crowd with holiday favorites near the announcer stand.

  4. 6:00 PM

    Parade Steps Off!

    Lights, floats, marching bands, dancers, classic cars, and more roll down Main Street.

  5. 7:15 PM

    Santa's Grand Finale

    Santa and Mrs. Claus close out the parade on the sleigh float, followed by the Community Tree Lighting.

  6. 7:30 PM

    Float Awards

    Awards announced for Best in Show, Most Whimsical, Best Lights, and the Peppermint People's Choice.

🎁 Enter a Float

Businesses, churches, schools, clubs, and families are all invited to join the parade. This year's theme is Candy Cane Lane, so think peppermint stripes, gumdrop glow, and gingerbread charm!

  • Entries are free for non-profits and $25 for businesses.
  • All entries must be decorated with lights. The more, the merrier!
  • Floats, walking groups, bands, horses, and vintage vehicles are all welcome.
  • Only Santa rides the finale float. Please leave your Santa suit at home.
  • Registration closes Friday, November 27, 2026.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What if the weather is bad?

The parade goes on in light snow or cold (it's Christmas in Kansas, after all!). In the case of severe weather, updates will be posted on Downtown Vision's social media pages by 2:00 PM on parade day.

Where should I park?

Main Street closes at 5:00 PM. Free parking is available on side streets and in the public lots east and west of Main Street. Please leave the parade route clear for floats and emergency vehicles.

Can floats throw candy?

Yes! Walkers may hand candy to spectators along the curb. For safety, please do not throw candy from moving vehicles, and remind kids to stay behind the curb.

Are pets allowed?

Leashed, well-behaved pets are welcome to watch the parade. Please keep them clear of horses and marching bands, and bundle them up if it's chilly.

How can I volunteer?

We need elves! Volunteers help with line-up, crowd guidance, and the Cocoa Stop. Reach out to Downtown Vision to sign up for a candy cane vest.
